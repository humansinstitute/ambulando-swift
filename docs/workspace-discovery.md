# Native Tower discovery

After sign-in, unlock or restored identity startup, Ambulando reads bounded kind-33356 self-index events from secure relays without an originating-app filter. Signature, author/recipient, singleton protocol/version/application tags, self NIP-44 plaintext and deterministic address are checked locally. The first `d` tag defines address reduction; the newest authentic replacement is selected before full validation, so an unreadable or malformed replacement cannot revive an older locator. Both flat and nested workspace identities work. Fresh signed `/me` must match all five locator identities before a Tower appears. Invalid, deleted, revoked or offline candidates do not remove other valid Towers.

Users choose a Tower, then choose an accessible workspace from its authenticated paginated list. Discovery does not select one automatically or replace local connections, content, drafts, preferences or other account/workspace partitions. Manual HTTPS entry and visible retry remain available.

After an explicit workspace choice, fresh membership is checked again and a compatible self-index is self-encrypted and signed by the unlocked user. The public-only originating namespace is shared with Android and documented in `app-identity.json`; it is separate from the locator app header and has no private scalar. Publication requires relay ACK and exact signed-event readback. Failure leaves the connection usable, with account/origin/app/workspace-scoped pending state retained in the local catalog and a retry action in Choose workspaces. Locked/background-read credentials never decrypt or publish indexes.

The real nostr-tools fixture in the core tests checks cross-language encryption, foreign app namespaces, two Towers, flat/nested locators and tombstones. Focused tests also exercise revoked/mismatched membership without persistence changes, malformed replacements, publisher namespace, and ACK/readback rejection. App builds and source delivery are separate from installation on Pete's devices.

The separate Grandparent Chat product retains its restricted family setup and does not enable these generic discovery/publisher controls. It requires separate product review before expanding its authority or connection flow.
